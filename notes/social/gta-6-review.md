## LinkedIn

GTA 6 reviews landed today, and the spread is one point: sixteen verdicts I could verify, from 9 to 10, averaging 9.8. Metacritic has it at 99 from 100 critics.
When everyone agrees on the score, the useful part of a review is the few sentences where the reviewer stops praising.

Rockstar Games' Vice City gets called the most convincing open world ever built in almost every one of them. Jason and Lucia's story is where reviewers spend their warmest paragraphs. The main story runs around 60 hours before side content.

The complaint that comes back is the frame rate. Rockstar confirmed last week that the game targets 30fps on every console, PS5 Pro included, and the Pro spends its extra power on PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows instead of a performance mode. VGC notes dips in the densest districts. One outlet calls the missing 60fps mode a flaw outright.

From the side of the people who build these worlds, that is a decision about what each frame is spent on. A city this busy is limited by the CPU running the simulation: traffic, crowds, police, weather that keeps its state across the map. A bigger GPU renders that faster; it does not make it tick twice as often. So the Pro's headroom went into light.

The reviews read like confirmation that the trade landed where Rockstar wanted it. Nobody describes the city as sparse. Several describe it slowing exactly where it is busiest.

For most players it will be the first thing they notice and not the thing they remember.
