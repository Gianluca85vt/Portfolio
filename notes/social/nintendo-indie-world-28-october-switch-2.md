## LinkedIn

Nintendo's Indie World runs at 15:00 Italian time today, and it is billed at
fifteen minutes. No games named in advance, which is normal for this format.

The line worth reading in the announcement is the one nobody treats as news:
indie games coming to Nintendo Switch 2 *and* Nintendo Switch. That conjunction
is the whole production problem for every team in the reel.

Switch 2 gives a small studio an Nvidia part with upscaling on it. For the first
time on Nintendo hardware, four people can pick a sensible internal resolution
and let the silicon carry the rest. The original Switch gives them a mobile part
that was already two years old the day that console shipped in 2017.

Shipping both is not one game with a quality slider. It is two LOD budgets, two
shadow resolutions, two texture sets, and a lighting rig that has to read the
same way at both ends — on a team that might be four people and a contractor.
Big studios pay the same bill; they just have a studio to spread it across.

So the thing I will be watching for at 15:00 is not the reveals. It is which
games are announced Switch 2 only. Each one of those is a team that sat down,
costed the month of work the 2017 machine needs, and decided it no longer
clears.

One caution on the footage. Thirty to sixty seconds a game, cut by Nintendo's
own editors, is not material you can judge performance from — the cuts land
where a frame drop would. Judge silhouette, colour and art direction, because
those survive a hard edit and compression. Hold everything about frame pacing
until somebody runs the game on real hardware.

Round-up goes up an hour after it ends.
