# The social kit — what the writer leaves with every draft

Write `notes/social/<slug>.md` in the same commit as the draft. The review
email shows it to Gianluca next to the Approve button, so he can post it the
moment the piece goes live. Format and reasons come from the growth plan of
5 October 2026.

```markdown
## LinkedIn

<the post>

## X

<post 1>

<post 2>

…
```

**No links anywhere in the file.** The email adds the article's address with
tracking tags itself: on LinkedIn it goes in the first comment, on X on the
last post. A link you write would be untagged, and on LinkedIn a link inside
the post costs it reach.

## LinkedIn

- **250-350 words that stand on their own.** The whole argument is in the post;
  a reader who never clicks still gets it. LinkedIn penalises posts that send
  people away, which is why the link lives in the first comment.
- **The thesis in the first two lines.** Everything after about 210 characters
  is behind "see more".
- Written as him, first person, in the voice of `notes/article-voice.md` — the
  banned construction is banned here too.
- **Name the companies and tools** the piece is about (Foundry, Maxon, Blender,
  the studio) so he can tag them: it is how vendors find the blog before it has
  to go looking for them.
- No hashtag wall. Two or three at the end, at most.

## X

- **A thread of 4 to 6 posts** that tells the technical finding in full: what
  happened, how it works, why it matters to the people who make things.
- **Each post under 270 characters.** The email counts them and flags any over
  280.
- The first post must work alone, as the hook. The last one ends on the point
  and leaves room for the link the email adds.
- Where a screenshot from the article would help a post, say which on its own
  line at the end of that post: `[image: shot-02.jpg]`.
- Write for the #b3d, VFX and gamedev crowd: specific, technical, no hype.

Bluesky needs nothing from you: it posts by itself when the article goes live.
