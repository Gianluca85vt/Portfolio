# Material for the Monday editorial

Gianluca: drop things here whenever they happen. A line is enough. Anything in
this file can be used in the editorial in your own voice, in the first person,
because you wrote it and it is true.

The editorial is written from your side of the industry, so the useful material
is the stuff nobody outside it sees: a job that took four times as long as the
estimate, a tool that broke in a specific way, a decision from above that landed
on you, something a client said, a thing you believed for years and then found
out was wrong.

It does not have to be dramatic. "Spent two days fixing a lightmap seam nobody
would ever have noticed" is more useful than an opinion.

**Nothing here is published verbatim** — it gets worked into an argument. And
nothing gets published at all until you approve the draft.
