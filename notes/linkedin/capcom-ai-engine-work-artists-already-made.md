Capcom said on Saturday that RE Engine is being evolved into an "AI-generation game engine". The same weekend, the creator of SFO: Grimhammer III deliberately corrupted his own mod so the AI reuploads of it would break.

Ten years of free work on Total War: Warhammer 3, wrecked on purpose, because that was the only lever he had.

Two answers to one question: who decides whether the generative thing is on, and what it gets pointed at.

Maxon answered it properly four days earlier. Cinema 4D 2026.4 ships an MCP server that installs disabled, runs local-only, folds model-driven edits into the undo stack, scopes tool permissions, keeps an audit log. That is the part a pipeline lead checks, and none of it will ever reach a marketing slide.

RE Engine is superb engineering and I'll say so without hedging. It is also the room thousands of people are contractually required to work in, and a direction announced upstream has no switch on it. An engine that generates has to generate from something, and that something is every asset anybody ever checked in.

A tool is a thing you can switch off. Anything you can't switch off is a working condition.
