Zerply goes dark at four o'clock this afternoon. Sixteen years of VFX credits and reels, switched off on a Monday, and the export window already closed on the 21st.

Five days after that deadline, MobyGames opened a tier that lets you claim the credits your studio left off the roll. Fifteen dollars a month.

The product is well designed. The meter is what I keep catching on. A hundred and eighty dollars a year to hold the receipt for work you already did, in a month where Microsoft cut just under half of World's Edge and a Gears story director posted that he was out days after the game went gold.

Fifteen a month is nothing when you are in work. Three months into a layoff it sits next to Substance and the domain renewal, and it loses, because it produces nothing this month. So the people who most need a verified record are the ones most likely to switch it off.

Transport Fever 3 ships tomorrow with its modders' work in the box. Urban Games paid those modders up front and put their names on the release. One line in a budget at a studio with a fraction of Microsoft's money.

Keep your own list. Screenshot your own work. Write down what you did while you still remember the specifics, on a machine you control.
