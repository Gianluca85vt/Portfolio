### Peter Medak, The Changeling and The Ruling Class Director, Dies at 88
Wed, 28 Oct 2026 16:57:21 +0000 — https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/peter-medak-dead-changeling-ruling-class-hannibal/

Peter Medak, director of the hugely influential horror film “The Changeling” and the Peter O’Toole-led comedy “The Ruling Class,” has died at 88. His family announced his death. Medak, who was Jewish, fled his native Budapest, Hungary, during the 1956 Hungarian Revolution, settling in England with his family. He began as a trainee in the British film industry ( described as “a long film industry apprenticeship that included work as sound editor, film editor, assistant director and second unit director”). In 1963, he signed a deal with MCA Universal Pictures to direct television films. By 1967, he had signed with Paramount to direct feature films. His debut feature, 1968’s “Negatives,” based on Peter Everett’s 1961 novel of the same name, was not well received upon its initial release. “The movie, which starts off as a sort of highly cinematic ‘Who s Afraid of Virginia Woolf?’ winds up as an episode right out of ‘Peanuts,’ with Snoopy, imagining himself as the gallant Red Baron, crashing to his death on top of his dog house,” scoffed The New York Times at the time. But like several of Medak’s films, “Negatives” gained appreciation in subsequent years, particularly through home-video releases. (Severin released it on Blu-ray in 2025.) Medak’s breakthrough arguably came just a few years later with 1972’s “The Ruling Class,” a pitch-black comedy starring O’Toole “as an insane British royal who believes he s the second coming of Jesus Christ,” as one retrospective review put it .
