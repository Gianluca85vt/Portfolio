is the one that carries the schedule.

**16:00 Italian time** — 7:00 PT, 10:00 ET, 23:00 JST — and it is billed at
roughly 45 minutes on Switch 2 games shipping this winter. Yesterday's was
the Zelda anniversary broadcast, thirty minutes on one series. This one is the

## What the framing tells you

Nintendo said winter and it said Switch 2. That is a narrower brief than the
usual September Direct, and the narrowness is the interesting part of the setup,
because the calendar it has to fill is unusually short.

Ocarina of Time landed on 5 November yesterday. GTA 6 is in the same month.
Whatever Nintendo shows in the next three quarters of an hour has to sit either
side of a game the rest of the industry has spent a year moving away from, and
there are only so many weeks in a winter to do that with.

Nintendo Treehouse: Live follows the broadcast with extended play of some of
what gets shown, which is usually where the actual detail is — a trailer tells
you a game exists and a Treehouse segment tells you how it moves.

I will have the round-up an hour after it ends.
