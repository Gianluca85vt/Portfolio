August is the lull. Everyone is saving their big swings for the autumn, which means the
calendar looks thin — and that is exactly why it is the best month of the year to actually
play something to the end instead of adding it to the pile.

Three worth your attention.

## Beast of Reincarnation — 3 August

This is the interesting one. It is **Game Freak** — yes, the Pokémon studio — making
something that looks nothing like Pokémon.

<figure>
  <img src="/img/blog/games-august/beast-02.jpg" loading="lazy" width="1440" height="810" alt="Emma and Koo in the overgrown ruins of post-apocalyptic Japan, from Beast of Reincarnation" />
  <figcaption>Post-apocalyptic Japan, and not a Poké Ball in sight. Screenshot: Game Freak, via the official Steam page.</figcaption>
</figure>

A sci-fi fantasy action RPG set in a cursed post-apocalyptic Japan. You play Emma, a
warrior, with a dog named Koo alongside you. Out on PC, PS5 and Xbox Series X.

I am always interested when a studio that could comfortably print money forever decides to
make something else instead. Those projects are either fascinating or a mess, and both are
more fun than another safe sequel.

## Metal Gear Solid: Master Collection Vol. 2 — 27 August

The second box set, landing on **PS5, Xbox Series X/S, Switch 2, Switch and PC** — which is
close to everything.

If you missed these the first time, this is the least painful way in. If you did play them,
you already know whether you are buying it. You are.

## Big Walk — August

**House House**, the studio behind Untitled Goose Game, is back with a co-op game about
walking and talking across a large open world.

That description sounds like nothing. So did "you are a goose and everyone hates you". This
is a studio that has earned the benefit of the doubt on premises that sound thin on paper.

## Also landing

**Final Fantasy XIV: Dawntrail** gets its Switch 2 port on 4 August, alongside the
base-building shooter **Once Human**. **Lies of P: Complete Edition** arrives digitally on
Switch 2 this month, with a physical release following in October.

---

Use the gap. From September onward you will not get another one until next summer, and the
backlog does not shrink on its own.
