**15:00 Italian time** — 14:00 GMT, 7:00 PT, 10:00 ET, 23:00 JST. Nintendo bills
it at roughly fifteen minutes of announcements and updates on indie games for
Switch 2 and Switch, and it goes out on Nintendo of America's YouTube channel.

That is the service part.

## Nintendo has named nothing

No games, no dates, no prices. The announcement ran the standard sentence —
roughly fifteen minutes of new announcements and updates on indie games coming
to Nintendo Switch 2 and Nintendo Switch — and stopped. Any lineup you read this
morning is somebody's guess dressed as a leak.

The last Indie World was in March. Several of the games it showed have since
shipped: Denshattack, a port of the twin-stick shooter Minishoot' Adventures,
and a shadow drop of Blue Prince on the day of the broadcast itself. That habit
of putting a finished game on the eShop before the stream ends is the argument
for watching live rather than reading the round-up.

One detail I could not confirm: a report that Nintendo's Japanese page lists the
domestic broadcast at around twenty-five minutes, against the fifteen quoted
everywhere else. I found it in one place and could not check it against Nintendo
directly, so fifteen is the number to plan around.

## Two consoles in one brief

The sentence says Switch 2 *and* Switch, and that conjunction is the hard part of
every game in the reel.

Switch 2 hands a small team an Nvidia part with upscaling on it — the first time
an indie on Nintendo hardware can pick a sensible internal resolution and let
silicon do the rest. The 2017 machine hands them a mobile part that was
already two years old the day the console shipped. Shipping both means two LOD budgets, two shadow resolutions,
two texture sets, and a lighting rig that has to read the same way on each, on a
team that might be four people and a contractor. Scale changes the size of that
bill and not its shape: [what porting Diablo 4 to Switch 2 costs the art team](/blog/diablo-4-switch-2-age-of-hatred-what-porting-costs-art/)
came down to the same arithmetic with a studio to spread it over.

So watch for which games are announced Switch 2 only. That is a team working out
that the old console is no longer worth the month.

## What the reel will not show you

Thirty to sixty seconds a game, cut by Nintendo's own editors, is not footage you
can read performance from. The cuts tend to land where a drop would. Judge the
silhouette work, the colour, the art direction — the things that survive
compression and a hard edit — and hold everything about frame pacing until
somebody runs the game on hardware.

Nintendo's winter is crowded already. [The September Direct laid out the Switch 2
schedule](/blog/nintendo-direct-9-september-switch-2-winter/) through the Ocarina
of Time remake in November, and fifteen minutes of indies is how the weeks
between those get filled.

Round-up an hour after it ends.
