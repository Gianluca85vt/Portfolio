## Why it is split in two

Sony's own announcement gives the first show the usual brief — updates from
PlayStation Studios and third-party partners — and gives the second one a
narrower one: deep dives into games from studios in Japan and Asia.

Splitting those is a decision about attention rather than about content. A
single showcase has to keep everyone watching through segments that were never
aimed at them, and the middle of a games showcase is where audiences leave. Two
shows with two briefs means the Japanese and Asian slate gets its own opening,
its own pacing and its own host, instead of six minutes somewhere in the second
half of a broadcast built around a different audience.

Whether that reads as promotion or as relegation depends entirely on what is in
the second show, which is a good reason to watch both rather than the
highlights reel.

## What closes the first one

The announcement says the State of Play ends with an extended look at the vast
world of Final Fantasy VII Revelation. Square Enix has had the closing slot
before and the phrase "extended look" usually means a long uncut section rather
than a cut trailer.

That matters more than it sounds for this game in particular. When the last
trailer showed a parachute, [the thing worth noticing was what a parachute
implies about the level](/blog/final-fantasy-vii-revelation-planets-crisis-trailer/):
you cannot drop a player onto a roof unless the roof is real geometry with a
walkable surface, a collision volume and a reason to be there. One traversal
verb rewrites the brief for every environment artist on the project.

So the thing to watch in a long uncut section is the ground. Specifically:

- Where the camera does not go. A showcase edit hides the seams between
  authored space and filler; an extended look has fewer places to hide them.
- How the world loads. Long traversal shots are where streaming shows itself —
  pop-in on shadows first, then on foliage, then on the small props that dress
  a street.
- Whether the rooftops are the same rooftops. Instanced modular kits are correct
  engineering and they read as repetition from the air, which is the angle a
  parachute puts you at.

That is what the work looks like from the inside, and a long take is the rare
occasion when you get to see it.

## The rest is unannounced

Everything else is unannounced by design, and the number of leaks and rumours
circulating this morning is not something I am going to relay as reporting.
Sony has said "surprise announcements and updates" and nothing more specific,
so anything more specific you read before 15:00 came from somewhere other than
Sony.

Worth setting a reminder for. The show runs at an unusual hour for Europe — the
middle of a working afternoon rather than late evening, which is where these
have often landed — and the two broadcasts back to back mean the interesting
half may well be the one that starts when the first one ends.
